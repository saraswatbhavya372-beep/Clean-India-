🌱 About Clean India

Clean India is a platform created to encourage people to keep their surroundings clean, reduce waste and protect our environment.

Together, citizens can report cleanliness problems, learn eco-friendly habits and volunteer for a cleaner and greener India. 🇮🇳

🌿 Cleanliness Tips

♻️ Separate dry and wet waste.

🗑️ Always use a dustbin.

🌳 Plant and protect trees.

🚯 Avoid throwing plastic on roads.

💧 Save water and keep water sources clean.
