🌿 Everyday cleanliness tips

♻️ Separate waste
Keep wet and dry waste separate according to local collection rules.
🗑️ Use the right bin
Do not leave litter beside an overflowing bin; report it to the local service.
🚯 Reduce single-use plastic
Carry a reusable bottle or bag when practical.
💧 Protect water
Never dump waste into drains, ponds or streams.
